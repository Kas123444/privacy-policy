Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Xyverix Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our Pinterest Pin Scheduler application and automation service.

Pinterest Pin Scheduler is a tool designed to help users schedule and publish Pins to their own Pinterest accounts through the official Pinterest API.

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling information responsibly and transparently.

1. Information We Access

When you connect your Pinterest account to our service, we may access limited information and permissions made available through the official Pinterest API and authorized by you.

This may include:

  • Pinterest account information necessary to connect your account.
  • Pinterest account or profile identifiers.
  • Information about Boards required to select a destination for a Pin.
  • Pin-related information necessary to schedule or publish Pins.
  • Access tokens required to communicate with Pinterest on your behalf.

We only request and use permissions necessary to provide the requested scheduling functionality.

We do not request or collect your Pinterest password, session cookies, or other Pinterest login credentials.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed through Pinterest is used only to provide the Pinterest Pin Scheduler functionality.

This may include:

  • Connecting your Pinterest account.
  • Allowing you to select a Pinterest Board.
  • Scheduling Pins that you choose to publish.
  • Publishing Pins at the time selected by you.
  • Maintaining the technical operation of the automation.
  • Troubleshooting Pinterest API or scheduling errors.

We do not use Pinterest API information for unrelated purposes.

3. User Control and Authorization

You remain responsible for the Pins you choose to schedule or publish.

The service does not independently decide what content should be published to your Pinterest account.

Users must choose the Pins and scheduling actions they want the service to perform.

You may revoke the application's access to your Pinterest account at any time through the applicable Pinterest account settings or authorization controls.

4. Pinterest API

Our service uses the official Pinterest API to provide Pinterest-related functionality.

Authentication and authorization are handled through Pinterest's authorized authentication mechanisms.

Our use of Pinterest API information is subject to Pinterest's applicable Developer Guidelines, Developer and API Terms, and other applicable Pinterest policies.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information used by our service.

API credentials, access tokens, client secrets, and other confidential credentials should not be exposed publicly or placed in public source-code repositories.

Our automation may use n8n as part of the technical infrastructure required to process scheduling requests and communicate with Pinterest.

Pinterest API information is handled only as necessary to provide the requested service and in accordance with applicable Pinterest policies.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade Pinterest API information.

We do not share Pinterest API information with third parties except where necessary to operate the requested service, comply with applicable law, protect our rights, or as otherwise permitted by applicable Pinterest policies.

We do not use Pinterest API information for advertising outside Pinterest.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only when reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality, maintain security, resolve technical issues, or comply with legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete or securely dispose of it.

If you disconnect your Pinterest account or request deletion of information associated with your use of our service, please contact us using the email address below.

8. Third-Party Services

Our service may rely on third-party infrastructure or services, including n8n and Pinterest, to operate the automation.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our service, legal requirements, or applicable platform policies.

When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date shown at the top of this page.
